FAQs
How do I transfer money?
›
Open Transfer from Home or the bottom menu. Select a saved recipient or enter a new account number/email, enter the amount and an optional note, then confirm. Transfers between Firmco accounts are instant; external transfers typically clear within 1–2 business days. Daily transfer limits apply based on your account tier.
How do I activate my card?
›
Go to Cards, tap your new or pending card, then select Activate Card. Confirm the last 4 digits shown in the app and set a secure PIN. Virtual cards are issued digitally and become active immediately after approval. You can manage limits, freeze, or view card details anytime from the Cards section in the app.
How do I increase my limit?
›
Your current account limit is $5,000,000. To request a higher limit, go to More → Settings → Security or contact support at firmcobank@gmail.com with your account number and reason for increase. Limit reviews typically complete within 1–3 business days and may require identity re-verification.
How do I dispute a transaction?
›
Open the transaction from Recent Activity, tap it to view the receipt, then choose Report an issue or contact support within 60 days of the transaction date. Provide the reference number, amount, and a brief description. Firmco will investigate and respond within 10 business days. Provisional credit may apply for eligible debit card disputes.
